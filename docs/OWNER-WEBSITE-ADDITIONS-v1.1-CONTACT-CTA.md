## 3. SERVICES — APPROVED SPATIAL PRESENTATION

Exact services:

1. Website Design & Development
2. Application Design & Development
3. AI Automation Design & Development
4. Advertising & Custom Video Design & Production
5. AI Tools — MOSAIX LAB

### Presentation

- Cards are vertical.
- One card is ACTIVE and dominant.
- Neighboring cards remain visible in perspective/depth.
- Active card is larger, more frontal and visually dominant.
- Overall feeling: premium, spatial, cinematic — not a generic carousel.

### Navigation

- Mouse wheel / scroll → previous or next service.
- Click visible side card → bring it to active position.
- Drag where appropriate.
- Swipe on touch devices.
- Arrow buttons are not required and should be removed when the interactions above are clear and reliable.
- Do not create scroll-jail behavior.

### Motion

Transitions should have controlled spatial weight/inertia. Appropriate properties may include translateZ, perspective, rotateY, scale and restrained optical/depth response. Avoid cartoon bounce. Active cards should settle with premium controlled motion.

### Video reveal

- Default card = clean premium glass; no obvious thumbnail.
- Corresponding service video exists behind/inside the card.
- Hover/pointer on active card optically reveals the video through the glass.
- Glass becomes more transmissive rather than abruptly swapping content.
- On leave, return elegantly to clean glass.

```text
SCROLL = EXPLORE SERVICES
CLICK = SELECT SERVICE
HOVER = REVEAL SERVICE FILM
```

Touch behavior needs a deliberate equivalent.
